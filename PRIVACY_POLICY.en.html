Privacy Policy — Tuner for All

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tuner for All (called Afina Tudo in Brazilian Portuguese), developed by Rafael Arriel, handles user data.

1. Data handled by the app

Tuner for All handles only the data required for the tuner to work:

  • Microphone audio: used in real time to identify the frequency, note, and cents deviation of the sound being played.
  • Local preferences: A4 reference frequency, tolerance, noise gate, display options, language, last mode, selected instrument, and tuning.

The app does not request a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, financial information, advertising identifiers, or account information.

2. Microphone use

Android requests microphone access only when it is needed for the tuner. Audio is processed locally and in real time on the device to calculate tuning.

Tuner for All does not record audio files, store audio samples, transmit audio to servers, or share audio with third parties. Users can stop capture at any time with the in-app microphone button or revoke permission in Android settings.

3. Local storage

The preferences described in this policy are stored only in the app's local storage on the device. They are used to restore settings and the last tuner in use.

These preferences are not sent to us or to third parties. Users can remove them by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling Tuner for All.

4. Collection, sharing, and advertising

Tuner for All does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive data off the device. It does not use user accounts, proprietary servers, analytics, telemetry, advertising services, advertising SDKs, or trackers.

Accordingly, we do not share, sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

5. Security

Because audio processing and preference storage occur on the device, the app does not transmit audio data over the network. Microphone access depends on the permission granted by the user in Android.

6. Children and teenagers

The app is not designed to collect personal data from children or teenagers and does not provide registration, user-to-user communication, or advertising.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Tuner for All's features or data handling practices change. The update date will be revised on this page. If an update introduces data collection or sharing, applicable permissions and disclosures will be updated before that processing begins.
